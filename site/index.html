Demo build. Volumes are sample data until the local inventory analyzer (Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Photos, Tasks, metadata only, nothing leaves your machine) is connected. The plan always ends in verification: source data is untouched until checksums and dates pass. Destination mappings follow the tribe-x.org alternative tools list: Drive → Proton, Gmail → Eureka.

EthiScore column and divested-stack scores come from the EthiScore dataset (v2026-10-31-draft); entries marked * are draft scores added for this kit, and Eureka is a draft placeholder pending verified data. The dataset is also served from this site at /api/v1/scores.json, the endpoint the EthiScore browser extension fetches.
